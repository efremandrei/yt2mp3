# yt2mp3 identity

Violet play symbol with a cut through its center and mint audio bars. Dark background #0F111A, violet #AD82FF, mint #68E8CD. The vector master is yt2mp3-icon.svg; Android assets live in app/src/main/res/drawable/brand_*.xml.

Launcher: adaptive foreground/background layers, legacy drawable, and a monochrome themed-icon variant for Android 13+. Keep the mark inside the central adaptive-icon safe area.

Launch: Android system splash on Android 12+, branded fallback on older versions, then the matching in-app preparation screen. No deliberate startup delay. The preparation screen disappears on success or error and restores the saved app skin. Device screenshots are included for review.
